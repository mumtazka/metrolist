Features

Everything a music app should be.

Built with Material 3, packed with the features streamers actually ask for — no ads, no premium tiers, no nonsense.

Background playback

Keep the music going while you chat, game or browse. Skip silence, set a sleep timer, fine-tune tempo and pitch.

Offline cache

Download songs and whole playlists for flights, commutes and dead zones. Your library travels with you.

Live synced lyrics

Word-by-word time-synced lyrics with AI-powered translation, right on the player screen.

Listen together

Start a real-time session with friends — same queue, same moment, wherever you both are.

Material You themes

Light, dark, black or dynamic color — plus 19 hand-tuned palettes. Make it yours in a tap.

Full audio control

System equalizer, audio normalization, precise volume gapless playback — tuned the way you like it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Metrolist really free?

Yes — completely. Metrolist is free and open source under the GPL, with no ads, no premium tier and no telemetry. Support the project by starring the repo or contributing code.

Which platforms are supported?

Android (APK), Windows (.exe) and Linux (.deb). A macOS build is not available yet.

Do I need a YouTube Music account?

No. You can search and play right away. Signing in is optional — it unlocks library sync for your songs, albums, artists and playlists.

Why doesn't it load in my country?

Metrolist streams from YouTube Music. If YouTube Music isn't available in your region, you'll need a VPN or proxy connected to a supported region.

How do I report a bug or contribute?

Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub. Every contribution — code, translations, bug reports — counts.
